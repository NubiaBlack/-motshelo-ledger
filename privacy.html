Motshelo Ledger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Motshelo Ledger is a digital record-keeping platform being developed to support Motshelo and savings groups in Botswana.

We recognise the importance of protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when the service becomes operational.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, information may include:

  • Names and contact details of group members.
  • WhatsApp telephone numbers and messages sent to the service.
  • Group membership and contribution records.
  • Loan, repayment and outstanding balance records.
  • Financial transaction dates, amounts and descriptions.
  • Account and subscription information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain group and member records.
  • Record contributions, loans and repayments.
  • Generate financial summaries and statements.
  • Respond to authorised user requests.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the platform.
  • Manage subscriptions and communicate service updates.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Motshelo Ledger is being developed with WhatsApp-based interaction in mind. If this feature is introduced, messages and relevant account information may be processed through WhatsApp and Meta's services.

WhatsApp and Meta process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies when using their services.

4. Access to Group Information

Group financial records are intended to support the administration of savings groups. Access will depend on a person's role and authorisation within the group. Treasurers and other authorised persons may have access to records necessary for their responsibilities.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the platform, such as hosting, messaging and technical infrastructure providers.

Information will not be sold as a product to third parties. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to protect the rights and security of users and the platform.

6. Data Security

We intend to implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting and record-keeping requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access to their personal information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion where appropriate.

Requests can be submitted using the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform develops or when our data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
