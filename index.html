What is Motshelo Ledger?

Motshelo Ledger is a Botswana-focused software project aimed at simplifying the way savings groups maintain and access their financial records.

Member Management

Organise group membership and maintain individual member records.

Contribution Tracking

Record member contributions and monitor payment histories.

Loan Records

Maintain records of loans, repayments and outstanding balances.

Financial Statements

Work towards clearer financial reporting and downloadable group statements.

Designed for Botswana

Motshelo groups play an important role in community savings and financial cooperation. We are developing a simple digital solution that supports the people responsible for maintaining these records.

Our goal is to make financial record-keeping more accessible, organised and convenient.
